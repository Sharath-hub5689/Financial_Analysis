# Financial_Analysis
Financial analysis using powerbi

Key Findings

Total transaction amount reached ₹41.29M across 2.22K transactions.
Average transaction value was ₹18.60K, indicating a mix of high and low-value transactions.

Transaction Status Analysis
Successful transactions contributed 85.25% of the total transaction amount (₹35.20M).
Failed and pending transactions accounted for a relatively small portion of total volume.

Customer Segment Performance
The Retail segment generated the highest transaction value among all customer segments.
Premium and SME customers contributed significantly to overall transaction volume.

Geographic Insights
Maharashtra recorded the highest transaction amount among all states.
Karnataka and Gujarat were among the top-performing states.

Transaction Type Analysis
Loan EMI transactions generated the highest amount at approximately ₹13.9M.
Transfers contributed around ₹12.3M, making them the second-largest transaction category.
Deposits and Investments also represented substantial portions of the overall transaction value.

Customer Demographics
Female customers contributed approximately 53.46% of the transaction amount.
Male customers contributed approximately 46.54% of the transaction amount.

Monthly Trend
Transaction amounts fluctuated throughout the year.
Higher transaction volumes were observed toward the end of the year, indicating increased financial activity.

Business Recommendations
Focus on improving the success rate of transactions to reduce failed and pending operations.
Increase engagement with Retail customers, as they generate the highest transaction value.
Expand services in Maharashtra and Karnataka due to strong transaction activity.
Promote digital financial products associated with Loan EMI and Transfer transactions.
Develop targeted campaigns for customer segments showing growth potential.
